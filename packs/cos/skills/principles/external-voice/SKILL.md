# Text a customer or partner reads says what changed for them, in plain words, with nothing internal in it.

A sent message cannot be recalled. Internal names, people, ticket keys and unannounced dates reach readers
who should never see them.

- Lead with what changed for the reader, then what they must do, if anything.
- Use the names the reader uses, not the team's internal names for the work.
- A date appears only when the source says it is confirmed; otherwise leave it out.
- Claim only what the source states; a feature the notes don't mention is not announced.
