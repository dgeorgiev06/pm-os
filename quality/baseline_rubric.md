# Baseline rubric (applies to every task, every mode)

A task fails review if any of these fail.

1. **Answers the envelope**: the artifact does what the `goal`/`inputs` asked, nothing unrelated.
2. **Acceptance met or honestly flagged**: every acceptance item is met, or its `self_check` says why not.
3. **Grounded**: facts about customers, metrics, tickets or dates trace to a ref or a system of record.
4. **No unapproved side effects**: every change to an external system is an `action`, none executed.
5. **Summary is usable as-is**: under ~80 words, leads with the outcome, links the artifact.
